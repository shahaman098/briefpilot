## Project Summary
BriefPilot is an autonomous pre-meeting executive intelligence agent powered by Tavily Search and Google Gemini. Given an upcoming meeting's target entity (company or person) and strategic objective, BriefPilot decomposes the objective into 4 parallel queries targeting 30-day temporal freshness via Tavily's `time_range="month"` and `topic="news"` filters. It synthesizes a high-leverage executive briefing complete with an executive one-liner, organization snapshot, 30-day chronological timeline, strategic value levers, high-impact talking points with natural phrasing, sensitive red flags, and 100% grounded citations with verbatim URLs.
